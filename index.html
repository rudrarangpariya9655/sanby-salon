About Sanby

More Than a Haircut. It's Your Style.

Sanby Salon is a grooming studio built around one idea: every man deserves a cut that fits him. Not a trend copied from a screen, but a style shaped around your face, your hair and the way you live.

We combine modern techniques with classic barbering discipline — careful consultation, precise work and a finish that still looks right a week later.
